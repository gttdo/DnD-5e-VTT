# Currency icons — credits

All 5 coin denominations share one face derived from **game-icons.net** (**CC BY 3.0**):
[crown-coin](https://game-icons.net/1x1/lorc/crown-coin.html) by Lorc, normalized to
`currentColor` at 24×24. The app tints each denomination (copper/silver/electrum/gold/
platinum) by color at render time; the silhouettes are intentionally identical.
